# 🚀 Application of Machine Learning

This document covers the real-life applications of Machine Learning, as outlined in the CampusX series.

---

## 📌 Key Applications Overview

### 1. Retail - Amazon / Big Bazaar
* Product recommendations, demand forecasting, and inventory management based on customer behavior.

### 2. Banking and Finance
* Fraud detection, credit scoring, algorithmic trading, and automated customer support.

### 3. Transport - OLA
* Route optimization, ETA (Estimated Time of Arrival) prediction, and dynamic surge pricing.

### 4. Manufacturing - Tesla
* Autonomous driving features, predictive maintenance of machinery, and quality control on assembly lines.

### 5. Consumer Internet - Twitter
* Content moderation, personalized feeds, sentiment analysis, and targeted advertising.
